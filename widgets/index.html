One lightweight app.
28 compact desktop widgets.

Built natively in Swift 6, SwiftUI, and AppKit with demand-gated polling (~0.5% idle CPU) and instant single-Desktop swipe pinning.

Time, Date, Weather & Calendar

Analog Precision, Digital Bento, Dual World Clock, World Clock Studio, Date Progress, Open-Meteo Weather, and Apple Calendar with full schedule timelines.

Sticky Notes & AI Refine

Resizable Quick Note, rich-text Note Pad, Notes List, All Notes window (⌘0), global capture hotkey (⌃⌥N), checklists, daily backups, and one-click AI actions.

Built-in Local AI Router

Mount AI Router Server or AI Router Chat to run a local OpenAI-compatible proxy on port 9694 across 6 free & paid providers with automatic failover.

AI Limits & Dev Tools

Live 5-hour session caps for Claude, Gemini, and Codex, plus Local Ports (open, copy, stop localhost servers) and PM2 process monitoring.

Tasks, Music, Launcher & Paste Board

Interactive Quick Tasks with Apple Reminders sync, Spotify & Apple Music Now Playing controls, customizable Quick Launcher tiles, and a 9-slot text/image Paste Board.

Google Sign-In & Firebase Sync

Sign in with Google to sync your Notes, Desktop widget layout, Settings, Quick Tasks, and Quick Launcher across Macs while keeping your Paste Board private and local.

Install in one command.

Available on the its-ash Homebrew tap or as a direct release zip.

Terminal 
# Add and trust the tap (once)
$ brew tap its-ash/tap
$ brew trust its-ash/tap
# Install Widgets
$ brew install --cask widgets

Direct download? Unzip, drag Widgets.app to Applications, then run xattr -cr /Applications/Widgets.app in Terminal so macOS Gatekeeper lets it open. Homebrew runs this automatically.
